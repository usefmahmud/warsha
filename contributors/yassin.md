# Yassin

![](yassin.png)

**Role:** Infrastructure & DevOps

I take care of everything under the app — containers, reverse proxies, backups, and a deploy path that has to behave the same way every single time.

My habit is to read before I touch: logs, disk, memory, then decide, because most "mystery" outages turn out to be something ordinary that nobody looked at.

A change I cannot undo is a change I do not make, and I would rather tell you "not yet" than hand you something that works only until the next reboot.

I write the steps down as I go, so the next person hits a known path instead of rediscovering it at a bad hour.
