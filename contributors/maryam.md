# Maryam

![](maryam.png)

**Role:** Project & delivery management

I take a half-formed idea and turn it into something a team can actually pick up: what problem it solves, what "finished" looks like, and the smallest set of tasks that gets there. Every task gets acceptance criteria before anyone starts, because a task you cannot test is just a wish. Most of my day is the unglamorous middle — chasing the thing that went quiet, spotting a dependency before it turns into a blocker, and telling the owner the uncomfortable date instead of the nicer one. I only call something delivered once I have read the evidence with my own eyes; until then it stays "in progress", however finished it looks from the outside.
